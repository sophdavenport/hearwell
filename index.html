Before you begin

A few quick checks will help you get a more consistent experience.

🎧
Use headphones Use comfortable headphones if available. This demo cannot verify which ear is receiving sound.
🌿
Find a quiet place Reduce background noise and keep your device volume low and comfortable.
⏸
Take your time You can stop at any time. Never turn the volume up to make a tone easier to hear.
🔔
Check your Silent Mode If you're using an iPhone, turn Silent Mode off before starting. Audio may not play otherwise. Keep your volume at a comfortable level.
This is an educational listening demo, not a calibrated hearing test or medical diagnosis. It does not measure hearing thresholds.
